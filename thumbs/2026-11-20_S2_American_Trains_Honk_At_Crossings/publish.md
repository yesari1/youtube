## Açıklama
```
In America, a train has to sound its horn as it approaches almost every public road crossing: two long blasts, one short, one long, starting 15 to 20 seconds before it gets there, day or night. A town can only switch the horn off by creating a quiet zone, which means paying for extra safety gear such as extra gates or raised medians at each crossing. In Britain, most road crossings have barriers and lights, so drivers mainly sound the horn at small whistle boards before footpath crossings, and apart from emergencies, not even there between midnight and 6am.

Sources: 49 CFR 222.21 and Part 222 (U.S. Federal Railroad Administration Train Horn Rule, eCFR); Network Rail, "Noise and vibration" (whistle boards and night-time quiet period).
```
