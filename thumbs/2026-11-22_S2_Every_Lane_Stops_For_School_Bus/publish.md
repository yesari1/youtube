Both lanes stop for a school bus in America. A viewer pointed this out under our jaywalking Short, so we checked: it's true. On a two-lane road, when a school bus puts out its stop arm and flashes its red lights, every car has to stop, in both directions, in all 50 states, because children step off and cross in front of the bus. Britain has no such law: the Highway Code only asks drivers to pass a stopped school bus slowly and carefully. And yet, in a single day, American bus drivers counted about 56,000 cars that didn't stop.

Sources: Education Week, "When Cars Have to Stop for School Buses, and When They Don't, in Every State" (2023); NHTSA, Reducing the Illegal Passing of School Buses; The Highway Code, rule 209 (gov.uk); NASDPTS National School Bus Illegal Passing Survey 2025-26.
