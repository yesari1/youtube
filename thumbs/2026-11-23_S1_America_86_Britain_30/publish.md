## Açıklama
```text
On the same hot day, a British thermometer says 30 and an American one says 86. Both are right. America still uses Fahrenheit, a scale from 1724, where water freezes at 32. Britain used it too, until its weather forecasts switched to Celsius in 1962, where water freezes at zero. America tried in 1975 with the Metric Conversion Act, but the law made switching voluntary, so thermostats, ovens and forecasts stayed in Fahrenheit.
```
