```text
Why American Houses Have Basements
```
Açıklama:
```text
In the northern United States, winter can freeze the ground up to five feet down, and frozen soil swells and lifts whatever is built on it. So a house there has to stand on foundation walls that reach below the frozen layer. Builders are already digging a deep hole, so they dig a little more and turn it into a room: the basement. In Britain the frost rarely gets deeper than about knee height, so a shallow footing is enough and there is no deep hole to begin with. In the warm American South, most new homes are built on a concrete slab without a basement.
```
