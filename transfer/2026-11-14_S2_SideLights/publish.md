Why American Cars Have Lights on the Side
```
## Description
```
Every new American car has small amber lights at the front of its sides and red ones at the back. They are side marker lamps, required by US federal rules for more than fifty years, so a car crossing in front of you at night shows its length and which way it is going. In Britain they are only required on long vehicles like lorries and buses; British cars just have a small indicator on the side that blinks when turning.

Sources: FMVSS 108 (49 CFR 571.108), side marker lamps; NHTSA DOT HS 806 430 (evaluation of side marker lamps); UN Regulation No. 48, section 6.18.
