Why American Highway Signs Are Green

## Açıklama
American highway signs are white on green, while British motorway signs are white on blue. In the late 1950s, US road officials put up test signs in blue, green and black over an unopened stretch of the Capital Beltway near Washington, and let drivers try them. 58 percent preferred green, and white-on-green was approved in January 1958. Britain, preparing its first motorway, considered following America's green, but chose blue because it stood out better against the green countryside.
