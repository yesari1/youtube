```text
Why British Ambulances Are Checkered
```
Açıklama:
```text
British ambulances wear a bright yellow-and-green checkerboard, while American ambulances are mostly white with a stripe. The pattern, known as Battenburg, was designed in the 1990s by British government scientists for police cars, with the aim that drivers could spot them from up to 500 metres. The high-contrast squares stand out by day and are reflective, so they shine in headlights at night. Ambulances adopted it in yellow and green. In the US, ambulance colours are mostly up to each service.

Sources: Home Office Police Scientific Development Branch Battenburg design brief (via Battenburg markings history); NHS England National Ambulance Vehicle Specification (2019); GSA Federal Specification KKK-A-1822F; NASEMSO model ambulance rules.
```
