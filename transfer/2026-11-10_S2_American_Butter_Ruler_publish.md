```text
Why American Butter Has a Ruler on It
```
Açıklama:
```text
American butter comes with a ruler printed on the wrapper, and it measures spoons. American recipes measure butter by volume, in cups and tablespoons, and cold butter does not fit neatly into a cup. So the wrapper does the measuring: each line is one tablespoon, and the eight tablespoons of one stick make exactly half a cup. British recipes ask for grams instead, so British butter is sold as one 250 g block that goes straight on the kitchen scale.

Sources: Land O'Lakes, Kitchen Reference: Measurements; Cabot Creamery, How Many Tablespoons in a Stick of Butter; Library of Congress, In Custodia Legis: Weights and Measurements; Food52, Scales make baking easier, so why are they not embraced in the US.
```
