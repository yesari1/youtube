## Açıklama
```text
For years, British shoppers typed a four-digit PIN, while Americans signed for the same chip card. When US banks added chips in 2015, most chose signature over PIN: issuing millions of new PINs was slow and costly, and customers already knew how to sign. Britain had gone the other way on 14 February 2006, when shops stopped taking signatures for most chip-and-PIN cards, and card fraud in shops fell sharply. In April 2018, the big US card networks told shops they could stop asking for a signature at all.

Sources: UK Finance (20 years of Chip & PIN); APACS chip and PIN progress reports (2006); Congressional Research Service R43925, The EMV Chip Card Transition (2016); Mastercard and Visa signature announcements (2017-2018).
```
