# Why American Freight Trains Are Miles Long

## Description
```
Some American freight trains run over two miles long, while Britain's longest freight trains stop at about 775 metres (roughly half a mile). In Britain, freight shares busy tracks with passenger trains and has to pull into passing loops (side tracks) to let them by, so a train can only be as long as the loop. In the US, freight railroads own most of the tracks, there is no federal limit on train length, and one crew (usually two people) can run a very long train.

Sources: US GAO report GAO-19-443 "Rail Safety: Freight Trains Are Getting Longer" (2019); FRA Safety Advisory 2023-03; National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, "Long Freight Trains" (2024); FRA Train Crew Size Safety Requirements final rule (2024); Association of American Railroads freight rail facts; Amtrak corporate profile; Global Railway Review (2021) and Railfreight.com (2020) on Network Rail's 775 m freight trains.
```
