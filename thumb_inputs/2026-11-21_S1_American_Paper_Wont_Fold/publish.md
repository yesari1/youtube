## Açıklama
```text
Fold a sheet of British A4 paper in half and you get A5: exactly the same shape, just smaller. That's because every A size is cut to the same ratio, so a copier can shrink one size onto the next without cutting anything off. American Letter paper (8.5 by 11 inches) doesn't work that way: fold it in half and it comes out long and skinny. The A sizes were first set in Germany in 1922 and became the world standard, but the US and Canada kept Letter.

Sources: ISO 216 international paper sizes (from DIN 476, Deutsches Institut für Normung, 1922); ANSI, standard paper sizes (US Letter).
```
