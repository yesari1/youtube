## Açıklama
Many American ladder trucks are tillers: about 57 to 63 feet long, with a second firefighter in a cab at the back who steers the rear wheels. They carry an aerial ladder that commonly reaches 100 feet (about 30 metres) for tall buildings, and the International Fire Code asks for fire access roads at least 20 feet wide. A typical British fire engine is about 8 metres long and carries its ladder on the roof, built for streets that are often centuries old.

Sources: Pierce Manufacturing, "Tractor-Drawn Aerial"; Utah Fire & Rescue Academy tillering handout; International Fire Code 503.2.1 and Appendix D (as adopted, e.g. Seattle Fire Code); Royal Berkshire FRS and Dorset & Wiltshire FRS fleet pages; Cheshire FRS FOI 2052 (appliance dimensions).
