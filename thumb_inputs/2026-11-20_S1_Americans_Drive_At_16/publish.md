## Açıklama
At 16, an American teenager can usually drive a car. A British 16-year-old can only ride a moped (a small scooter limited to about 28 mph); the car has to wait until 17, a rule that goes back to the Road Traffic Act of 1930. In America there is no single national age: each state sets its own, and rural farm states have long let teenagers start early. In South Dakota you can get a learner's permit at 14, as long as a licensed adult sits beside you.

Sources: GOV.UK "Vehicles you can drive" and "Riding a motorcycle, moped or motor tricycle"; Road Traffic Act 1930, section 9 (legislation.gov.uk); South Dakota Department of Public Safety, "Teen Drivers"; IIHS graduated driver licensing table.
