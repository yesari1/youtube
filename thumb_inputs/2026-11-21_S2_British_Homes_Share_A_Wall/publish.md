More than half of British homes are joined to the house next door: a semi-detached house shares one wall, a terraced house usually shares two. In America, about six in ten homes stand completely alone, with a gap on every side. Why? Britain's towns boomed in the 1800s around the factories, and building houses in a row packed more homes onto less land, right by the work. America had land to spare, and later, cars to reach it.
