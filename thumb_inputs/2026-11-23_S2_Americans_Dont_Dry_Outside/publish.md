In Britain, washing goes on the line. In America, it usually goes in the dryer. Over eight in ten American homes have a clothes dryer (2020); in England it's fewer than six in ten (2017). And in some American neighbourhoods, a washing line can even break the rules: homeowners' associations decide how a street looks, and some ban clotheslines as an eyesore. Florida's law says they can't ban them, and several other states passed "right to dry" laws too.
