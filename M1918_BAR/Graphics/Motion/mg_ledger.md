Rule notes:
- Each graphic differs from the previous one in at least three of layout / camera / reveal / accent (see rows).
- G01 and G06 are both timelines, but four graphics apart and different in camera, reveal and units (seconds vs years).
- Timing follows the real narration (word timestamps from VO03/VO05/VO06/VO07/VO08). In G01, G03, G04, G05 and G06 the last spoken data point arrives at 65–95 % of the slot, so the build ends later than the usual ~60 %. Every element appears on the word that names it and is readable for at least 1.5–2 s before the cut.
- Graphics were built entirely in code (HTML/Chromium, 2× internal resolution, Lanczos downscale, grain), because ChatGPT on the PC was not reachable. No generated imagery; the BAR silhouette is a simplified drawing.
- SFX: procedurally synthesised (ticks, soft thuds, whooshes, chimes, typewriter keys), no third-party samples; ≈ −30 LUFS.
